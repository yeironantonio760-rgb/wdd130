Dominican Rapids

Our mission is to create exciting and safe rafting adventures. Join us to explore beautiful rivers, enjoy nature, and make unforgettable memories with friends and family.

History

Dominican Rapids began with a group of friends who loved rivers and outdoor adventures in the Dominican Republic. Their shared enthusiasm inspired them to create a place where others could experience the beauty and excitement of our rivers.

Today, our team shares that passion by helping visitors discover the excitement of white water rafting. We value teamwork, respect for nature, and memorable experiences with friends and family.
